## 1. To Have (20 min)

|                                            | Affirmation +                      | Negation ⊖<i class="r"></i>                        | Question ?                                                 |
| ------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| ![[English Pronouns Subject Me.jpg\|100]]  | I <i class="p">have</i>            | I <i class="r">don't ⊖</i> <i class="p">have</i>   | <i class="y">Do</i> I <i class="p">have</i>?               |
| ![[English Pronouns Subject You.jpg\|200]] | You <i class="p">have</i>          | You <i class="r">don't</i> <i class="p">have</i>   | <i class="y">Do</i> you <i class="p">have</i>?             |
| ![[English Pronouns Subject She.jpg\|100]] | He / She / It <i class="b">has</i> | She <i class="r">doesn't</i> <i class="p">have</i> | <i class="y">Does</i> he / she / it <i class="b">have</i>? |

Asking questions:
- How many arms do <i class="p">you</i> have?
- I <i class="p">have</i> 2 arms.

- How many pens do <i class="p">I</i> have
- You <i class="p">have</i> 1 pen.

- How many eyes does <i class="b">he</i> have
- He <i class="b">has</i> two eyes.

### Activity: How Many Do You Have? (10 min)
By using the ==balloon== with body parts select students and they have to ask "How many \_\_\_ do you have?" where \_\_\_ is pointed by you, the teacher.
