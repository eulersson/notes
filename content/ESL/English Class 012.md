## 1. Review: Months (5 min)
Distribute the students in a circle holding hands. One says the name of a month, and claps the next who says the next month and so on. After doing it forwards do it backwards too.

### Activity: Asking Next Student (20 min)
First do the demonstration with two students in front of the class.

By rolling a dice one student needs to ask that question to the student next to him. After the student replies, rolls the dice and go on.

> [!NOTE] Efficiency
> We can split the class in two so the other teacher could do the other half.

**While the activity goes on, stop and ask every student after their turn is finished "How old is he?", referring to a previous student, and they must say "He is thirteen years old.", that way they work on using the third person too.**

- What ~~is your~~ ==is his== name?
	- ~~My~~ ==His== name is \_\_\_.
- How old ~~are you~~ ==is he==?
	- ~~I am~~ ==He is== \_\_\_ years old.  
- Where ~~are you~~ ==is he== from?
	- ~~I am~~ ==He is== from \_\_\_.
- How ~~are you~~ ==is he==?
	- ~~I am~~ ==He is== \_\_\_.
- How ~~do you~~ ==does he== feel?
	- ~~I feel~~ ==He feels== \_\_\_.
- What ~~am I~~ ==is he== doing?
	- ~~You are~~ ==He is== \_\_\_.
