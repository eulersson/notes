## 1. Review: Prepositions (15 min)
Only do 2 students per each group, the rest of words speak them aloud.

| Set          | Way  | Points         |
| ------------ | ---- | -------------- |
| Prepositions | Type | Yes if 2 right |
| Emotions     | Type | Yes if 2 right |

## 2. Months (5 min)
Drill them and tell them next day they will clap them in a circle (students hold hands in circle and as one says one month claps the next player who will say the next one).

> January, February, March, April, May, June, July, August, September, October, November, December.

## 3. Prepositions (10 min)
### Activity: Placing Objects
Do one or two round of [[English Class 008#Activity Placing Items Around (30 min)]]

- Put a pen on Manjil's head
- Put a sharpener outside the room.
- Put a pencil between two pillows

- Put a pillow near the wall.
- Put a table in front of the door.
- Put a chair on the table.

- Put a book between curtains
- Put a sandal in the cupboard
- Put a whiteboard behind a dust bin.
