 **(1) What is your name?**
 My name is \_\_\_.
 
 **(2) How old are you?** 
 I am \_\_\_ years old.
 
 **(3) Where are you from?**
 I am from \_\_\_.
 
 **(4) How are you?**
 I'm (not) \[good / fine / OK / alright\] thank you, and you?
 
 **(5) How do you feel?**
 I feel \_\_\_
 **Oh, you feel \_\_\_**
  🎲[^1] = happy (1) / sad (2) / tired (3) / sick (4) / relaxed (5) / angry (6) [^2].
 
 **(6) What are you doing?**[^1] 
 I am \_\_\_ing
 **Oh! You are \_\_\_ing**[^3] 
 🎲[^1] = talk (1), sweep (2), read (3), jump (4), fly (5), brush \_\_\_ (6)

[^1]: Roll dice to select one emotion.
