I was impressed during [[Theravada Meditation for Vajrayana Monks|the meditation class a monk was teaching in Pema Tsal]] where he would speak about **confidence** and **power** that could be achieved by [[Iddhipada Meditation]].

In the case of the practice he prepared for it, the first one is related to mind-body coordination:

![[Theravada Meditation for Vajrayana Monks#^61f97b]]

What I loved is how he demonstrated the aspect of confidence in relation to mind-body coordination:

> Rise your left arm. *(students do without doubt)* Rise your left arm. *(students do without doubt)*. \[Repeats: left arm, right arm, ...\] Now **try to raise your left arm!**.

When he said ==try to== raise your left arm, it confused me, so I didn't know what to do. Same with the rest of students. It was a great example to illustrate confusion that might arise between the link of body and mind.
