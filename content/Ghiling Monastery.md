Small monastery that currently has 7 monks, of which most of them are studying in India.

![[Ghiling Monastery Khenpo Ngawang Tashi.jpg|Khenpo Ngawang Tashi and the Ghiling Monastery]]

The villagers asked for a *khenpo* to bring back the monastery to life: renew it, set up a school, guesthouse, advertise it, ... And [[Khenpo Ngawang Tashi]] has been appointed for it.

![[Ghiling Monastery Stupa.jpg|Stupa at the Ghiling Monastery]]
