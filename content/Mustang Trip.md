From August 19th to August  25th 2026 I visited Upper and Lower Mustang with [[Khenpo Ngawang Tashi]].

## Altitude

 Drove from Pokhara (800m altitude) to [[Lo Manthang]] (3800m), that's an increase of 3000m!

To prevent altitude sickness it's best to not go beyond 500m after passing 3000m. 

Didn't have altitude sickness symptoms other than peeing a lot and slight preassure in head and eyes.

I woke up so many times the first night, each time clearly remembering the dreams but since I didn't write anything down I lost them at early morning except for one where I was waiting to receive a Sakya name from [[Sakya Trizin Rinpoche]], which in fact I commented to [[Khenpo Ngawang Tashi]] about, and he said he could arrange it. Let's see, it would be auspicious.

## Sakar Zang

> ས་དཀར་བཟང་།
> (ས་ “sa” from ས་སྐྱ་ “sakya”)

Recently high lamas from [[Lo Manthang]] requested to weekly celebrate Tibetan culture (mainly circle dances) in community on Friday — day 41st [[Sakya Trizin Rinpoche]] was born. It's an initiative very similar to *lhakar* (white wednesday) but without the political aspect. 

Here's a video before I joined them. The steps look simple but I struggled to find the pattern and loop it.

![[Lo Mantang Sakar Zang.mp4]]

[[Khenpo Ngawang Tashi]] tells me they started doing this very recently, perhaps June/July 2026.

## Thubchen Monastery

Built around 15th century by [[Ngorchen Kunga Zangpo]], founder of one of the [[3 Sakya Subsects]].

## Muktinath

Area with various temples workshipped both by buddhists and hindus.

![[IMG_6493.jpeg]]

The hindus see it as the abode of Vishnu. And for buddhists it's seen as a land where daikinis and [[Guru Rinpoche]] practised.

The element of water purification is present: there are 108 water taps arranged in a semicircle as well as two ponds. Devotees run under the extremely cold 108 waters and then dio into those ponds — one for Lakshmi (wealth, prosperity) and one for Saraswati (wisdom). After “having washed impurities” or “sins” they cloth and proceed into the temple.
