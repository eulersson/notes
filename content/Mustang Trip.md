From August 19th to August 25th 2026 I visited Upper and Lower Mustang with [[Khenpo Ngawang Tashi]].

## Altitude

 Drove from Pokhara (800m altitude) to [[Lo Manthang]] (3800m), that's an increase of 3000m!

To prevent altitude sickness it's best to not go beyond 500m after passing 3000m. 

Didn't have altitude sickness symptoms other than peeing a lot and slight preassure in head and eyes.

I woke up so many times the first night, each time clearly remembering the dreams but since I didn't write anything down I lost them at early morning except for one where I was waiting to receive a Sakya name from [[Sakya Trizin Rinpoche]], which in fact [[Khenpo Ngawang Tashi]] is arranging for me in real life. Very auspicious.
