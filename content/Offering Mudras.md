## Explanation

The eight, often associated with Seven Limb Prayer and ancient Indian hospitality, are: 

- **Argham** (Water for Drinking): Symbolizes purity, clearing obstacles, and offering refreshment.
- **Padyam** (Water for Washing): Symbolizes cleaning the feet, representing purification of negative karma.
- **Pushpe** (Flowers): Symbolizes beauty, openness, and the blossoming of positive qualities.
- **Dhupe** (Incense): Symbolizes fragrance and moral discipline.
- **Aloke** (Light / Lamp): Symbolizes wisdom that dispels darkness and ignorance.
- **Ghende** (Perfume / Scented Water): Symbolizes purification and the scented water used for cleaning.
- **Naivedya** (Food): Symbolizes spiritual food and nourishment, representing the accumulation of merit.
- **Shabda** (Music): Symbolizes delightful sounds, representing the offering of sound and wisdom.ཨོ
