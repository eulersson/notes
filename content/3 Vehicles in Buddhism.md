ཐེག་པ་གསུམ། — the three *yānas* ("vehicles"), three scopes of motivation and method, each one containing the previous.

| Vehicle | Tibetan | Goal | Method |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Hinayana** (lesser vehicle) | ཐེག་དམན། | Personal liberation from samsara — arhatship | Renunciation, ethics, analysis of the [[4 Noble Truths]] |
| **Mahayana** (great vehicle) | ཐེག་ཆེན། | Buddhahood for the benefit of all beings | Bodhicitta, the six perfections, emptiness |
| **Vajrayana** (diamond vehicle) | རྡོ་རྗེ་ཐེག་པ། | The same buddhahood, in one lifetime | Tantra: empowerment, deity visualization, mantra |

Vajrayana is not a fourth goal but a swift set of methods **within** Mahayana, which is why Tibetan Buddhism is Mahayana in view and Vajrayana in practice.

*Hinayana* is a label coined by Mahayana authors and carries a dismissive tone; **Theravada** is the respectful name for the living tradition. The [[Bodhisattva Vows|bodhisattva vows]] explicitly forbid denigrating it.
