*infinite space, infinite consciousness, vacuity, peak of existence*

The formless realm has **four** divisions, in ascending subtlety:

| #   | Name                                                                | Sanskrit                             | Tibetan         | Object of absorption        |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------ | --------------- | --------------------------- |
| 1   | Sphere of Infinite Space                                            | Ākāśānantyāyatana                    | ནམ་མཁའ་མཐའ་ཡས་  | Limitless space             |
| 2   | Sphere of Infinite Consciousness                                    | Vijñānānantyāyatana                  | རྣམ་ཤེས་མཐའ་ཡས་ | Limitless consciousness     |
| 3   | Sphere of Vacuity (Nothingness)                                     | Ākiñcanyāyatana                      | ཅི་ཡང་མེད་      | "There is nothing"          |
| 4   | Sphere of Neither Perception nor Non-Perception (Peak of Existence) | Naivasaṃjñānāsaṃjñāyatana / Bhavāgra | སྲིད་རྩེ        | Subtlest possible cognition |

The fourth — *Bhavāgra*, "peak of existence" (སྲིད་རྩེ་, *srid rtse*) — is the highest possible rebirth in saṃsāra.
