## 3 Natures

Anything that comes in mind must fall into one of two categories:

- Non-Existent
- Existent
	- Impermanent
	- Permanent
		- Ultimate Reality
		- Permanent other than ultimate reality


From those elements “Which 4 items cannot be divided any further?” — non-existence, impermanence, permanent reality and permanent phenomena other than permanent reality (those are the leaves of the nested list).

On the other side there's the *3 natures*: **imputed nature**,  **other-powered nature** / **dependant nature**,  **thoroughly established nature**.

Now we must match the two lists:

| Anything that comes to mind             | Three Natures                 |
| --------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- |
| Impermanent                             | Other Powered Nature          |
| Ultimate Reality                        | Thoroughly Established Nature |
| Non-Existent                            | Imputed Nature                |
| Impermanent Other than Ultimate Reality | Imputed Nature                |

During the **first** Turning (four noble truths exists truly) he meant *Other-powered phenomena and thoroughly established nature exists truly* and in the **second** (nothing exists truly) *Imputed nature does not existing truly*
