## Heart Sutra

> TADYATHĀ (OṂ) GATEY GATEY PĀRAGATEY PĀRASAṂGATEY BODHI SVĀHĀ
_> Go, go, go utterly beyond, go, and establish enlightenment!_

“From from tears to fearlessness!”, or “Become happier and happier”.

The yardstick for our success in our spiritual practice is: **as we age to become happier and happier**.

## Purpose of Life

**The Purpose Of Life**: (1) minimize misery (2) maximize meaningful happiness (ease, fullfillment).

## Learn Slowly

> Learn! Don't be too quick to accept, don't be too quick to reject (unless **you are convienced**, through analysis).
> _— Geshe Dorji Damdul La_

Absorb, work on knowing, rather than judging. “Oh it doesn't make any sense” is **knowledge destroyer**.

## Why Dedicating

Just as when winning money, we think about how we use this money. Same with this knowledge. *In what way are we going to action this result?* 

## Compassion

(Compassion = Being of service to others) = Being of service to yourself

## Overusing Buddhism

A lady was trying to provoke Geshe-la by announcing she abandoned Buddhism. Geshe-la was answering “It's OK” all the time, but she kept repeating her decision — clearly expecting a *why?* Then Geshe-la asked *why*. “Because I have been praying the Buddha and other deities and I was expecting help with X, Y and Z and neither of these things happened”. Geshe-la says:

> If you are sick go to the doctor, don't go to the Buddha!
