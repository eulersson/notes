| #   | Truth                  | Group 1 — Identification                               | Group 2 — Practice                       | Group 3 — Results                                                                   |
| --- | ---------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| 1   | Suffering              | 1. This is the truth of suffering\*.                   | 5. Suffering is to be **identified**.    | 9. Although it is to be identified, there is **nothing to be identified**.          |
| 2   | Cause of suffering     | 2. This is the truth of the cause of suffering.        | 6. The cause is to be **abandoned**.     | 10. Although it is to be abandoned, there is **nothing to be abandoned**.           |
| 3   | Cessation of suffering | 3. This is the truth of the cessation of suffering.    | 7. Cessation is to be **actualized**.    | 11. Although it is to be actualized, there is **nothing to be actualized**.         |
| 4   | Path                   | 4. This is the truth of the path leading to cessation. | 8. The path is to be **meditated upon**. | 12. Although it is to be meditated upon, there is **nothing to be meditated upon**. |
\* Refers to [[3 Types of Suffering#^07e8c6|Pervasive Conditioned Suffering]]

**Group 3 has two readings:**
- *Conventional truth* — once enlightenment is reached, the work is already complete for that being, so there is nothing left to do.
- *Ultimate truth* — the four truths are empty of inherent existence; "nothing" points to emptiness.
