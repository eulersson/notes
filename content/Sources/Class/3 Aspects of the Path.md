*renunciation, bodhicitta and wisdom of emptiness*

The three principal aspects of the path are: **renunciation**, **bodhicitta** and **wisdom of emptiness**, they are connected as follows:

Remember the 2 [[Goals and Obscurations#^34535b|goals]] (*nirvana* and *budhahood*) and the potentials that they have and the 2 **obscurations** that hinder them:

![[Goals and Obscurations#^34535b|goals]]

## Motivations

To remove  the [[Goals and Obscurations#^34535b|2 obscurations]] we need: 
1. **Renunciation**: the passion to want to come out of painful tears (*afflictive obscurations*).
2. **Bodhicitta**: the passion to want to experience infinite ease (*cognitive obscurations*).

## Path

To materialize those aspirations we require the **liberating path**, we need to engage in actions that help us **remove ignorance**, effectively this is achieved by realizing the [[Wisdom of Emptiness]]: to **see the reality as it is**.
