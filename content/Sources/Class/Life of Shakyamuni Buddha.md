## Life of the Buddha

Don't start about about the miracles and great achievements. Instead start from his humanness.

1. Did prince Siddhartha really stop the (painful) tears?
2. If yes, how did he succeed at this?

Example: *Many people have **tinnitus**! They want to know about each other's stories and methods of improvements.* This is **wisdom** — to know where to look for: experience, and knowing they really speak from experience.

Prince Siddhartha could see injustice, nasty conflicts. Not about religion, it's about reality. Kingdom fights, wars. Then he thought further than his own kingdom, “How can I help people” (1. **motivation**).  Staying in the palace was not an option, since even within it there was also injustice that couldn't be completely mitigated.

He didn't know the way, so he searched teachers. The first was acharya **Alara Kakama**. He reached the same level of realization as the teacher. Siddhartha was very confident with his level of meditation. The student Siddhartha therefore was guided to another teacher,  **Udraka Ramaputra**. He also achieved his level of realization. 

“The problem of all beings are still there, I need to find another solution”

**6 years following an ascetic way.** After that, decided the **middle way** (no ascetism; no luxury) is most convenient. The wisdom of emptiness is realized in the **middle way**.
