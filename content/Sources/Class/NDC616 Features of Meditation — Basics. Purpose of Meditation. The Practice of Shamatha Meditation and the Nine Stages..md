## Single-Pointed Meditation

About single-pointed meditation:

1. **Body posture** ([[Buddhism 7 Point Posture of Vairochana]])
2. **Focal point**
	1. Breathe in, breath out.
	2. 21 cycles (or 5 minutes ≈ 60 cycles)
	3. If lost count, restart from 0. 
	4. Multi task it with tiny white dot visualization between nose and upper lip, 2-3-4mm diameter.
3. Identifying errors of meditations
	1. Mental laxity and excitement.
4. Applying remedies to overcome the errors
	1. **Introspection**: keep an eye on the mind to see what it is doing. Vigilance. Spying on your mind. Once it notices big changes it hands the task to *Mindfulness* (see below).
	2. **Mindfulness**: the rope. Tying it to the pillar.
	3. Both remedies help help increase *reactivity* but does not help with *productivity*.
