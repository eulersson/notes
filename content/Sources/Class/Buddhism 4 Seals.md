- **Impermanence** (_anicca_ Tib. དེ་རིང་མེད།): All compounded things are transient.
- **Suffering** (_dukkha_ Tib. སྡུག་བསྔལ།): All conditioned phenomena are unsatisfactory.
- **Non-self** (_anatta_ Tib. རང་དབང་མེད།): Nothing possesses an independent, permanent self.
- **Nirvana is peaceful** (མཐོང་བ་བདེ་བ།).
