1.  **Impermanence** (_anicca_\* Skt. अनित्य Tib. དེ་རིང་མེད།): All compounded things are transient.
2. **Suffering** (_dukkha_ Skt. दुःख Tib. སྡུག་བསྔལ།): All conditioned phenomena are unsatisfactory.
3. **Non-self** (_anatta_ Skt. अनात्मन् Tib. རང་དབང་མེད།): Nothing possesses an independent, permanent self.
4. **Nirvana is peaceful** (*nibbanam santam* Skt. निर्वाणं शान्तम् Tib. མཐོང་བ་བདེ་བ།).
<small>* cursive is Pali language</small>

Help dispel the [[4 Invesions (Ignorances)]].
