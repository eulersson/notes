> Ceasing of karmas and afflictions is liberation.
> Karmas and afflictions arise from conceptual thoughts.
> These arise from elaboration (of grasping at true existence).
> Elaboration ceases by (or into) emptiness.

## Extracting 5 Elements

**Nirvana** being *free* from pain; **Samsara** lack of *freedom*. Opposite of samsara is nirvana.

*Karma* (contaminated ones) is why we are in *samsara*. 

For *liberation* we need to identify the *immediate cause* of: \[contaminated\] *karmas* and *afflictions*. 

What is the *immediate cause* of *karmas* and *afflictions*?
- *conceptual thoughts* or of *inappropriate attention*.

What is the *immediate cause* of *conceptual thought*?
- *Elaboration* of *self-grasping ignorance*

We need to know how these 5 are cause-related so miseries I go through cease. We need to know how these 5 come into being.

1. Samsara
2. Contaminated karmas
3. Afflictions
4. Inappropriate attention
5. Self-grasping ignorance

## Dream Example

> *Example*. I'm asleep, I'm dreaming I am in Tibet House Delhi attending class. In the class I pick up the phone. I look very carefully *Oh, this is a brand new iPhone 17 Pro Max*. You try to get it but another person comes fast to pick it up from you. You feel angry and start a fight with that person, which happens to be a kid. You go to jail. 

*Why are you in prison?*  Because of the fight.
*Why did you fight?*  Because I want the phone and another person wanted to take it out from me.
*Why are you angry at the competitor?* Because I'm attached to the mobile.
*Why are you attached to the mobile?* Because the iPhone is amaaaaazing.

| Element                                                            | Example                                 |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------- |
| Being in \[dream\] prison                                          | Samsara                                 |
| Fight                                                              | Contaminated karma                      |
| Attachment to mobile                                               | Affliction                              |
| Aversion to competitor                                             | Affliction                              |
| Exaggeration of the qualities of the mobile                        | Inappropriate attention                 |
| Seeing the dream phone as a real phone, misconceiving it's reality | Misconceiving the reality of the object |

*If you didn't want to end up in prison what should have been done?* Not getting into fight → turning down affliction (not having attachment to the phone nor aversion to opponent) → not exaggerating the qualities of the object or having inappropriate attention → not being ignorant → wake up.

> If you want to stop the pains of samsara **wake up[^1] from the sleep of ignorance**.

In [[Goals and Obscurations]] we spoke about afflictions and contaminated karma, we should now understand *Inappropriate attention* and *Self-grasping ignorance*.

## Inappropriate Attention

When we see something we can identify: **object** + **attributes / qualities**.

When you misconceive...
- ...object → **ignorance**
- ...attribute / quality → **inappropriate attention**
	- Exaggerating negatively
	- Exaggerating positively

[^1]: Hence why the Buddha is referred as the fully awoken one.
