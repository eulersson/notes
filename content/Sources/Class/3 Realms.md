Skt. tridhātu, Tib. ཁམས་གསུམ་ 

| #   | Realm                                      | Sanskrit  | Tibetan        | Defining trait                                                                             |
| --- | ------------------------------------------ | --------- | -------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| 1   | Desire realm                               | Kāmaloka  | འདོད་ཁམས་      | Beings driven by desire for the five sensory objects; seek happiness outside.              |
| 2   | Form realm                                 | Rūpaloka  | གཟུགས་ཁམས་     | Beings turned inward through meditative concentration; subdivided into four dhyāna levels. |
| 3   | [[4 Formless Absorbtions\|Formless realm]] | Arūpaloka | གཟུགས་མེད་ཁམས་ | Meditative absorption beyond form — the subtlest realm of saṃsāra.                         |

| Suffering                                   | Desire | Form                                         | Formless |
| ------------------------------------------- | ------ | -------------------------------------------- | -------- |
| Manifest suffering (suffering of suffering) | ✓      | —                                            | —        |
| Suffering of change                         | ✓      | ✓ (1st–3rd concentrations only, not the 4th) | —        |
| Pervasive conditioned suffering             | ✓      | ✓                                            | ✓        |
