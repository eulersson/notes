[[Lobsang Tsering]] explained me about the etymology of Buddhist cast names.

**Tamang** comes from རྟ་དམག *tamag*, *horse warriors*, when later on its pronunciation changed to *tamang* instead of *tamag*.

**Gurung** originates from སྒོ་སྲུང་། *gosung*, *gate keepers* or *gate protectors*, they originally were hired to do such tasks. Also pronounced སྒོ་ཧྲུང་བ།.

He also pointed me how the demonym particles are a bit different across countries, they can be པོ་ པ་ བ་:

- བལ་པོ། *Nepali person*
- བོད་པ། *Tibetan person*
- རྒྱ་གར་བ། *Indian person*
