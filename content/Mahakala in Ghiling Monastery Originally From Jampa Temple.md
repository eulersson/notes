The king of [[Lo Manthang]] had two sons: one with the princess and another one with a villager. The royal son would stay to keep ruling [[Lo Manthang]]. The other son  he sent him at the age of 8 years old to rule Ghiling — there was no king back then.  As [^1]dowry he would send the Mahakala from [[Jampa Temple]]

[^1]: On marriages or similar situations, when offering the wife, gifts are send alongside to compensite for the responsability of taking care of her.
