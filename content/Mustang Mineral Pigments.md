Many monuments, houses, monasteries and other buildings are painted often with four colours.

Those are natural mineral pigments they get from the mountains. It's easy to see in the landscape mountains naturally shifting their colors:

- Gray
- Orange / Yellow
- Red
- White

In [[Nyiphug Namdrol Norbuling Monastery]] I received a book that had an article on that.
