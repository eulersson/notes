Tibetan: བླ་རུང་སྒར། (see [[Tibetan Gar Means Settlement]]).

In Sêrtar (色达县), Garzê Tibetan Autonomous Prefecture, Sichuan.

Founded by  Kyabje Khenchen [[Jigme Phuntsok]] (**Serta Larung Five Science Buddhist Academy**  in 1980, with less than 12 students).

The academy is a non-sectarian study center with around 10,000 monks, nuns and lay people.

The abbot [[Khenpo Tsulrim Lodrö]], it's abbot, is very known worldwide, as he travels and is interviewed.
