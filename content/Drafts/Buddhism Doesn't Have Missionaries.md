Other religions might encourage spreading the word and teachings that conform it. Buddhism seems to disallow it, and it has been explicitly said that Buddhism cannot be taught unless someone requests it.

That's why when Buddha was enlightened he says he went back to the woods, instead of spreading the precious teachings. It was some time after that Indra and Brahma prompted him *Please turn the Wheel of Dharma*.
