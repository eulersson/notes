Reflection and questions on [[Jigme Khyentse Rinpoche – The Unbearable Tenderness of Being]]
## Maturity

Tenderness does not have the layers of delusion of *anger* and *attachment*, but it might be imbued with *ignorance*. Are we saying tenderness in the sense of something we want to protect because it's vulnerable, innocent, devoid of negative aspects, is what we should direct ourselves towards? Wouldn't that be a *puer aeternus*  symptom?
