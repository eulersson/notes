In many religious traditions while the initiator (founder, leader, ...) of the movement is alive there's peace. But after his or her death, years pass and there's wars. Why? — Because of **interpretations**.

The case with the Buddha is a bit different because he was re-interpreting his own teachings, as we see in the various [[NDC600 Notes — Introduction#^8bf25e|Turnings of the Wheel]], not only that but to adapt it to the existing audiences ([[NDC600 Notes — Introduction#^faefc2|4 Schools]]).
