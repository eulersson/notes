I was very happy to see how a Theravada *bikkhu* was teaching meditation (theory and practice) to school monks at [[Pema Tsal]]. There was mutual respect and the initial chanting was in the corresponding tradition: first the monks did took refuge in Tibetan and then the *bikkhu* in Pali and similarly when dedicating the merits.

![[Theravada Teaching Vajrayana.mp4]]

The *bikkhu*, founder of the [Center for Right View Meditations](https://www.crvmhimalayas.org/) , developed training and programs to implement meditation daily into the life of students (monastic and non-monastic).

He structured a 20 minutes daily practice for them composed of:

- *5min* **Mind-Body Coordination** exercises taken from martial arts like *Tai Chi* (see video below for the full exercise). Breathing in an out must be coordinated with the movements and the exhalation involves bringing your lips together so to produce a wind-like sound.
- *5min* **Ear Consciousness Awareness Meditation** to focus on the sounds around us.
- *5min* of **Vipassana Body-Scan**, always equanimous, not liking or disliking the sensations, from top to bottom and bottom to top.
- *5min* of  **Anapanasati**, observing the breath: how the air goes in an out the nostrils and the touch of the breath upon the upper lip.

![[Theravada Teaching Vajrayana Mind Body Coordination.mp4]]

I was impressed at how well presented the class was, with all types of meditations and their purposes.
