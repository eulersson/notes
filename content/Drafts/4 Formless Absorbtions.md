The formless realm has **four** divisions, in ascending subtlety:

| # | Name | Sanskrit | Tibetan | Object of absorption |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Sphere of Infinite Space | Ākāśānantyāyatana | ནམ་མཁའ་མཐའ་ཡས་སྐྱེ་མཆེད་ | Limitless space |
| 2 | Sphere of Infinite Consciousness | Vijñānānantyāyatana | རྣམ་ཤེས་མཐའ་ཡས་སྐྱེ་མཆེད་ | Limitless consciousness |
| 3 | Sphere of Nothingness | Ākiñcanyāyatana | ཅི་ཡང་མེད་པའི་སྐྱེ་མཆེད་ | "There is nothing" |
| 4 | Sphere of Neither Perception nor Non-Perception (Peak of Existence) | Naivasaṃjñānāsaṃjñāyatana / Bhavāgra | འདུ་ཤེས་མེད་འདུ་ཤེས་མེད་མིན་སྐྱེ་མཆེད་ | Subtlest possible cognition |

The fourth — *Bhavāgra*, "peak of existence" (སྲིད་རྩེ་, *srid rtse*) — is the highest possible rebirth in saṃsāra.
