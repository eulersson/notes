> ཉི་ཕུག་རྣམ་གྲོལ་ནོར་བུ་གླིང་, often translated as “Sun Cave Monastery”.

![[Nyiphug Namdrol Norbuling Monastery Main.jpg]]

Lowo Khenchen Sonam Lhundrub (1456–1532), a scholar of the Sakya tradition and a prince of a Mustang kingdom (yes, there were many) found a monastery in ruins. Next to it there were caves and he stayed in meditative retreat. Afterwards we decided to rebuild the monastery.

### Speech to Encourage Speaking Tibetan

![[Nyiphug Namdrol Norbuling Monastery Entrance.jpeg]]

Karma, the principal of the monastery invited us to have lunch with the community and asked me if I could run a [[Motivation Tibetan Speech]] for the small monks to encourage them to speak more Tibetan among them. He thought if a westerner that's learning it speaks of how beneficial the language is they might take it more seriously than if the principal recommends them.
