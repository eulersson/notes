**Alexander the Great**. Refer as “great” because of how expanse was his empire. At time of death he wanted to leave 3 wills, each to exemplify special meaning:

| Will                                                    | Meaning                                   |
| ------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------- |
| Only physicians (not daughters) should carry his coffin | Even the best doctors can’t prevent death |
| His wealth should be scattered along the path           | Wealth is meaningless at the end          |
| His hands should hang outside the coffin                | We leave the world empty-handed           |
