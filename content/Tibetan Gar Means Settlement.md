[[Alexandra Sukhanova]] says in [[Alexandra Shukanova's Kham Travel]] that the early *Nyingmapas* used heavily this term སྒར། when settling around famous, charismatic, realized spiritual masters, this is how great monasteries came to be.

That happened for instance around the cave where [[Tsongkhapa]] meditated. People settled around him. Same in [[Larung Gar]].
