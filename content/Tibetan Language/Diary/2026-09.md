##  Similar Words for Problem
#needs-teacher 

Both རྙོག་གྲ་ and དཀའ་ངལ་ can mean “trouble”, we know them in their use རྙོག་གྲ་ཚ་པོ་ “troublesome, with complications” and དཀའ་ངལ་སྤྲད་པ་ “to face difficulties”.

Can we use དཀའ་ངལ་ with the non-verb *to have*? What about རྙོག་གྲ་? “There weren't any problems” is what I want to say.

## About When
#needs-teacher 

This question is related to [[Tibetan Connectives of Simultaneity#དུས་]].

Saying ང་ལ་དངུལ་==མེད་དུས་==ཟ་ཁང་ལ་==འགྲོ་གི་མེད།== could be any of those and therefore we would need to pay attention to the context?
- When I didn't have money I didn't (frequently) go to the restaurant
- When I don't have money I don't go (frequently) go the restaurant
- When I will not have money I will not be going (frequently) to the restaurant

I guess  ང་ལ་དངུལ་==མེད་དུས་==ཟ་ཁང་ལ་==ཕྱིན་མེད།== is more explicit in specificity instead of connoting habitual action.

### Stable

In Anki I keep tripping on the spelling of *tenpo*. I interchange them many times:

- ==བརྟན་==པོ་ *stable, firm* (*brtan* = solid, firm, constant, stable, steady, stead fast, firm position, everlasting, self-confidence, to stabilize)
- འཕྲོད་==བསྟེན་== *health* (*'phrad* = to fit, agree with; *bsten* = cultivation, honoring, to attend, serve, follow, study with, take, get, associate with, rely upon, on, enjoy, resort to, adhere to, keep, to follow \[a teacher\])

### Thin
