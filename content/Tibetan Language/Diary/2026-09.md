## Confusinon

### Stable

In Anki I keep tripping on the spelling of *tenpo*. I interchange them many times:

- ==བརྟན་==པོ་ *stable, firm* (*brtan* = solid, firm, constant, stable, steady, stead fast, firm position, everlasting, self-confidence, to stabilize)
- འཕྲོད་==བསྟེན་== *health* (*'phrad* = to fit, agree with; *bsten* = cultivation, honoring, to attend, serve, follow, study with, take, get, associate with, rely upon, on, enjoy, resort to, adhere to, keep, to follow \[a teacher\])

### Thin

It seems the སྲབ་སྲབ་ in deck [First 1000 Words](https://www.small-steps-tibetan.com/first-1000-words) of **Small Steps Tibetan** is not recognized every time I use it [[Pema Tsal]], they instead recognize ཀྲབ་ཀྲབ better.
