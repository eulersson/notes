## 2025-06-27
སྤྱི་ཟླ་དྲུག་པའི་ཚེས་པ་ཉི་ཞུ་རྩ་བདུན་རེད། ཉིན་གུང་ལྔ་པ་ཡོལ་ནས་སྐར་མ་བཅུ་རེད།

### Observations
To say suggestions after conditionals we usually say **could** :"if like X the you could do Y". Today at the tutor class I realized they use  ་་་ཐུབ་ན་ཡག་པོ་ཡོད་རེད to convey that meaning: ཁྱེད་རང་གི་གྲོགས་པོ་ལ་གུང་སེང་ཡོད་ན་་་ ཁྱེད་རང་དང་ཁོང་གཉིས་གླིང་ག་ལ་་་ ཕེབས་ཐུབ་ན་་་ ཡག་པི་ཡོད་རེད།

### Confusions
#### with me, with him
Another struggle I have is saying "with him", "with me", etc... I tend to use མཉམ་དུ་ when sometimes saying ་་་དང་་་གཉིས suffices.

#### feeling adjectives versus feeling verbs
To describe feelings sometimes it's confusing. Since for instance you say ང་སྐྱིད་པོ་འདུག and not སྐྱིད་པོ་གི་འདུག because it's not a verb, but ཐང་ཆད་ (tired) means **to feel tired**, therefore I keep making the mistake of saying ཐང་ཆད་འདུག instead of saying ཐང་ཆད་ཀྱི་འདུག

#### Impossible Conditional then-clause: Present or Future?
In page 83 they show us the impossible conditional versus present conditional selection based on how probable that case is to happen. I still struggle to select the correct auxiliaries for the else-clause. But it makes sense.

In those examples first they use the present-impossible (I think) དགོས་ཀྱི་ཡོད་ས་རེད། (ཡོད་ས་རེད། expressing probability) and on the example with the case being realistic they use the future དགོས་ཀྱི་རེད།

#### What auxiliaries go with ཡག་བྱུང་ and ཡག་རག་ #needs-teacher

We learned about:
- ཟ་ཡག་རག་ To have the chance to eat
- ཟ་ཡག་བྱུང་ To manage to eat

But how do we conjugate them?

Translate the following:
- ཡག་རག
	- Past
		- I had the chance to eat momos.
		- Did you have the chance to eat momos.
		- He had the chance to eat momos. (testimonial)
		- He had the chance to eat momos. (inferential)
	- Future
		- I will have the chance to eat momos.
		- Will you have the chance to eat momos?
		- He will not have the chance to eat momos. 
- ཡག་བྱུང་
	- Past
		- I managed to eat momos.
		- Did you have the chance to eat momos.
		- He managed to eat momos. (testimonial)
		- He managed to eat momos. (inferential)
	- Future
		- I will manage to eat momos.
		- Will you manage to eat momos?
		- He will not manage to eat momos.

#### Observations

#### Magical N In བྱེད་འདོད་
Just as ཤེས་འདོད་ is often read *shendö* the same happens with བྱེད་འདོད་ *jendö*

#### Verbalizers for དཀའ་ལས་
I got confused because the same base word is used དཀའ་ལས་ but the meaning changes dramatically with the verbalizer:
- དཀའ་ལས་རྒྱག་པ་ make an effort
- དཀའ་ལས་བཟོ་བ་ give a hard time
- དཀའ་ལས་ཕད་པ་ to encounter difficulties

#### Noun and Adjective Order
You can flip the order of the noun and adjective and throw a connective particle in there. It doesn't sound as natural but it's grammatically correct!
- ཁ་ཚ་པོའི་ཁ་ལག་
- ཁ་ལག་ཁ་ཚ་པོ་
