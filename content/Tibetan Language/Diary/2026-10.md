## ་་་ཟེར་ཡག་དེ།

When my guru explains concepts he uses sooooo much the construction ་་་ཟེར་ཡག་དེ། which I believe introduces the explanation that's going to follow on the topic before the word ཟེར་, like when he is explaining me the triple gem: དགེ་འདུན་དཀོན་མཆོག་ཟེར་ཡག་དེ། and then explains something related to it.

It reminds me of ག་རེ་རེད་ཟེར་ན། or simply the topic marker ནི།

## What Is The Difference Between རྗེས་ལ་, རྗེས་མ་ and རྗེས་མར་ When Not Using Time Units

## ཞེ་ས་ Shadows Meaning

I don't like using གནང་ in ཞེ་ས་ because if shadows the real meaning, forcing you to require more context to figure out the original verb. སྤྲོད་པ། གཏོང་བ། བྱེད་པ། and many more collapse into གནང་བ། That adds more arguments to my preference for not using respectful forms, since it also slows our speech.
