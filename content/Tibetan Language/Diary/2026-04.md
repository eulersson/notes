## Notes from Lesson 17's Key-Point Video

-  Honorific Head Pronunciation: དབུ། sounds *u*, not *bu*!
- Tibetans say དང་ཤོས། (*the very first*) sometimes instead of དང་པོ་ (*first*)! “firstest”!
