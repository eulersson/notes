![[Tibetan Colloquial Disclaimer]]

Regularly means *only*, *merely*
- ཁམས་གསུམ་སེམས་<i class="y">ཙམ་</i>མོ།། *The three realms are merely \[a manifestation of\] mind.*
- མི་བརྒྱ་<i class="y">ཙམ</i>། *About 100 people.*

But **after** the **root** of an **adjective** it means *a bit more X*.

> [!ATTENTION] Pronunciation
> ཙམ་ doesn't get pronounced fully, often you hear a little "ts" to the *preceeding adjective*. E.g. མགྱོགས་ཙ་ means *a bit faster*.

མོ་ཊ་མགྱོགས་<i class="y">ཙམ་</i>གཏོང་རོགས་གནང་། *Please drive the car <i class="y">a bit</i> faster! (H)*

### ཙམ་ By merely

When **added** to a **verb** in the **past tense** it means *by only...* or *merely having done X...*.

བློ་བསང་གིས་སྒྲོལ་མ་ལགས་ཐེངས་མ་གཅིག་མཐོང་<i class="b">བ་</i><i class="y">ཙམ་གྱིས་</i>རྦད་དེ་སེམས་པ་ཤོར་སོང་། *<i class="y">By only</i> seeing Dolma-la once, Lobsang fell completely in love with her.*
