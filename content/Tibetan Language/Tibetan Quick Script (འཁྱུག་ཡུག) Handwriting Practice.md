See [[Tibetan Script]] for an overview on the #tibetan scripts.

I have been fascinated by the འཁྱུག་ཡིག (quick script), or *cursive* hand, they use when writing on paper in everyday situations. I started looking for learning resources and in this page I compile some.

## Books

The education department of the Tibetan government published a good workbook available for free on their [website](https://bodyiglobjong.com/%E0%BD%9A%E0%BD%B4%E0%BD%82%E0%BD%A6%E0%BC%8B%E0%BD%A0%E0%BD%81%E0%BE%B1%E0%BD%B4%E0%BD%82%E0%BC%8B%E0%BD%A6%E0%BE%A6%E0%BE%B1%E0%BD%BC%E0%BD%84%E0%BC%8B%E0%BD%91%E0%BD%BA%E0%BD%96%E0%BC%8D/).
