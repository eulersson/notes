There are three popular #tibetan  script categories:
- [U-chan](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Uchen_script) (དབུ་ཅན) (with head)
- [U-me](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Um%C3%AA_script) (དབུ་མེད) (headless)

Within བདུ་མེད། there are more sub-script types:
- ཚུག་རིང་། (long form, slow calligraphy)
- ཚུག་ཐུང་། (short form, suitable for handwriting)
- འཁྱུག་ཡིག (fast letters, punctuation blends)

For Sanskrit mantras very often the [[Ranjana Script]] (Lantsa) is used.
