To practice [[Tibetan Relative Clauses]] Franziska ལགས་ came up with a great drill-kind of exercise to practice relative clauses by:

| You have                    | She gives you                                                                                                 | You have to                                                               |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| A full simple main sentence | A sentence adding more information to an agent, object, place                                                 | Integrate it into the simple main sentence without changing its structure |
| A full simple main sentence | The core sentence the main sentence has to end up like, alluding to an object (direct/indirect), agent, place | Adapt the main sentence so it ends with what she gave you.                |
