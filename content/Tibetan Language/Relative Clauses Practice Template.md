To practice [[Tibetan Relative Clauses]] Franziska ལགས་ came up with a great drill-kind of exercise to practice relative clauses by:

| You have                    | She gives you                                                                                                 | You have to                                                              |
| --------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| A full main sentence        | A sentence adding more information to an agent, object, place                                                 | Integrate it into the full main sentence without changing its structure. |
| A full simple main sentence | The core sentence the main sentence has to end up like, alluding to an object (direct/indirect), agent, place | Transform the original main sentence turning it into a relative clause.  |

## Changing Main Clause

| ------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
