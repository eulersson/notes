Few ways to incorporate new words:

1. Using the ལོག་ཡིག (flipped letters): ཁེ་ཎ་ཌ། (Canada)
2. རྗེས་གྲུབ། Name based on what the meaning is: གློག་ཀླད། (computer, lit. electric brain).
3. འདོད་རྒྱལ། Arbitrarily like ཆུ། (water).
4. Understand the two parts and render those into Tibetan སེམས་མཁམས་རིག་པ་བ། (སེམས་མཁམས། psychic རིག་པ། logic).

Initiatives like [[Emory-Tibet Science Initiative (ETSI)]] are constantly thinking about those.
