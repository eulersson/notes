Practice getting faster at [[Tibetan Table Modal Verb Conjugations|conjugating modal verbs]] by spinning the wheel! Play solo or with friends. Review all the conjugations by pressing `d` (or clicking *Answers*). The conjugation tables extracted from Franziska's method are in [[Tibetan Table Modal Verb Conjugations| here]], for more information on Franziska's methodology check out [The Heart of Tibetan Language](https://www.heartoftibetanlanguage.com/).
