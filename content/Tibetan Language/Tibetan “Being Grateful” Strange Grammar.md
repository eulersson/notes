It's funny when wanting to express you are grateful (བཀའ་དྲིན་) to someone you place the ལ་དོན། to yourself!

ང་<i class="g">ལ་</i>ལུག་ནག་པོ་ལགས་<i class="r">ལ་</i>བཀའ་དྲིན་ཆེན་པོ་ཞེ་དྲགས་ཡིན།

[[Franziska Oertle|Franziska-la]] gives us the mnemonic of thinking of *being kind to* as opposed to *being grateful*, and the ཡིན། would still work because despite speaking of someone else (non-egophoric) it still has a strong effect or relation to you.
